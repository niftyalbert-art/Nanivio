name: Communication grant periods

## Owner-defined communication offering

Normal peer-to-peer chat without Langpretation must remain free. Chat text and voice-note Langpretation require a paid subscription. Normal peer-to-peer audio/video calls are free for the initial 30 days; the following 30-day period requires a subscription with administrator-defined minutes. Call Langpretation gets a limited free-minute test, then requires subscription, covering both translated captions and translated audio. These rules and allowances must be editable in the administrator dashboard.

**Why:** The user defined this commercial separation between ordinary chat, ordinary calls, and Langpretation.

**How to apply:** Keep these entitlements distinct and do not let an expired calling or Langpretation allowance block ordinary chat. Do not assume the old catalogue's recurring free translation allowance expresses this policy. Preserve purchased benefits when moving users to revised plans; free-plan switches must not refill trials.

The initial 30-day ordinary audio/video calling period starts on the user's first successfully connected call, not successful sign-up.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected “First connected call” as the trial start.

**How to apply:** Ringing, unanswered calls and failed connections do not start the period. Persist the start per account so reconnecting or signing in again cannot reset it. Keep this calendar-time window separate from actual consumed call minutes and the Langpretation trial.

## Paid communication purchases

Paid communication tiers must use verified Paystack payment, not the former free allowance selector. A paid purchase can grant a new purchased term; unpaid/free-plan switches must still preserve the existing term and consumption, and independently purchased minute packs remain separate.

**Why:** The user requested working Paystack payments for top-ups, Langpretation, and subscriptions. Treating priced plans as free selections bypasses the intended payment.

**How to apply:** Keep free-plan selection separate from verified external-payment fulfillment. Do not debit a wallet to fulfill an external payment. A one-off term purchase is not a recurring mandate: do not advertise or enable automatic renewal without implementing actual provider-authorized recurring billing.

## Unpaid self-service changes

Unpaid communication plan switches preserve consumed allowance and the original entitlement period, even when changing the displayed billing cycle.

**Why:** Selection collects no payment. Creating a fresh grant on each switch allows unlimited provider-backed translation by alternating tiers or cycles.

**How to apply:** Renew self-service grants only after the existing term expires. Adjust a current term's quota against accumulated consumption, without extending expiry. Keep independently purchased minute packs separate.
