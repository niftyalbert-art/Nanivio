---
name: Global product direction
description: User's audience, visual direction, and scope boundary for the Nanivio refresh.
---

Nanivio is international, not only for Ghana, although its roots are related to Ghana/Africa.
The user rejected the white-and-green refresh because it “looks more as blur.”
The user selected a Bybit-inspired look and button/navigation style for Nanivio:
dark charcoal backgrounds, clear white text, gold primary actions, and compact
labeled controls. The direction remains an “authentic, classic and modern look,”
with crisp, readable communication screens, more room for calls and full-page chat, secondary
controls grouped under one menu, and a clear Send button. Chat should behave like
a real WhatsApp-style dialog, including sending images, videos and documents,
with “no fakes.”

For the landing and chat pages, the user's newer image reference supersedes
the sharper Bybit treatment: warm charcoal, champagne/cream accents, rounded
dimensional cards, pill selections and circular controls. Keep the generous
conversation area, clear typography and no-blur/no-fake-activity constraints.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked both pages to copy that reference's style and
to see completed image reviews.

**How to apply:** Preserve this scoped landing/chat direction during future
refreshes rather than reverting them to the older angular gold treatment.
Present source-based desktop/mobile reviews; clearly label any sample chat data.

The user reiterated that the persistent “Direct & Group Chats, Community Channels”
card must be removed, with those destinations available under one click. The
conversation and multiline composer must have full usable space, including visible
emoji entry. Both ringing and connected audio/video views should minimize written
clutter and keep secondary controls together.
The user repeated that the chat page still looked short and asked to enlarge it
like WhatsApp. Prioritize usable conversation height over a multi-row global
header, outer padding, or a centered maximum-width card.
The user further asked for the chat box features “exactly as WhatsApp”; use its
familiar conversation and composing interactions while preserving Nanivio's identity.
Calls should open straight into the phone dialer, with clear audio/video choices.
Billing and plan cards belong in More → Billing, not in Calls. Home should be a
full modern “Global Communication Platform” screen, not a large promotional card.
The user asked to maintain the Calls section's colour across the entire app,
including Chat's colour and layout. Use that shared palette consistently; keep
the full WhatsApp-style conversation/composer interactions. Malvi feature work
comes afterwards.

**Why:** The user clarified the international audience, rejected the washed-out
white/green refresh, and approved bringing the Bybit-inspired direction to Nanivio.
They repeated the space and mobile-readability requirements after finding clipped
words and features in the refreshed app.

**How to apply:** Do not restore the washed-out, blurred mint-and-white direction.
Do not require Ghana-specific geography at signup, fabricate users,
credentials, messages, call activity, or availability. Preserve working communication
behavior when changing presentation. Malvi brain and visualization completion comes
after the signup and app refresh, not as an unrequested expansion of this work.
