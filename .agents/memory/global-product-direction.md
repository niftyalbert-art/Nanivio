---
name: Global product direction
description: User's audience, visual direction, and scope boundary for the Nanivio refresh.
---

Nanivio is international, not only for Ghana, although its roots are related to Ghana/Africa.
The user rejected the white-and-green refresh because it “looks more as blur.”
The current direction is an “authentic, classic and modern look,” with crisp,
readable communication screens, more room for calls and full-page chat, secondary
controls grouped under one menu, and a clear Send button. Chat should behave like
a real WhatsApp-style dialog, including sending images, videos and documents,
with “no fakes.”

**Why:** The user explicitly clarified the audience and asked for this product direction.

**How to apply:** Do not restore the washed-out, blurred mint-and-white direction.
Do not require Ghana-specific geography at signup, fabricate users,
credentials, messages, call activity, or availability. Preserve working communication
behavior when changing presentation. Malvi brain and visualization completion comes
after the signup and app refresh, not as an unrequested expansion of this work.
