---
name: Nanivio administrator operations
description: Distinct administrator workspace, subscriber reporting and safe Malvi minute adjustments.
---
The administrator dashboard must look different from an ordinary user account.
Administrators need total-user, subscribed and unsubscribed counts and the ability
to credit or debit Malvi minutes for monthly and annual terms.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested these capabilities and a distinct
administrator experience.

**How to apply:** Keep operational controls separate from customer navigation.
Derive whole-account totals from authoritative records, never the visible
directory page. Distinguish active paid subscriptions from trials and sponsored
grants, rather than counting every active entitlement as paid revenue.

Manual minute adjustments are authorized administrator actions, not payment
confirmations. Preserve existing purchased term dates and consumption; already
used or reserved minutes cannot be debited.

**Why:** Support adjustments must not silently replace purchased benefits or
fabricate a paid transaction. A lost response can occur after an adjustment
commits, so reporting “nothing changed” can cause duplicate credits.

**How to apply:** Require an attributable reason and duplicate-safe operation
identity. Recover an uncertain result using the same operation rather than
instructing the administrator to create another grant. Installing administrative
support must not itself change anyone's balance.
