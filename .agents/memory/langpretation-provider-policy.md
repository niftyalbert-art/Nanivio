---
name: Langpretation provider boundaries
description: Owner-approved Palabra adoption and separation from Malvi and specialist African routes
---

Use Palabra for international Langpretation speech, without replacing Malvi's
working Gemini voice/microphone or the Khaya/Sunbird specialist language routes.

**Why:** The owner had Azure account problems and chose Gemini first, but live
international recognition/translation requests could not be completed reliably.
After a bounded Palabra speech test worked in both directions, the owner approved
connecting Palabra to Langpretation. This is not approval to change Malvi.

**How to apply:** Keep speech-to-speech routing separate from standalone text
translation and specialist-language pivots. Do not claim Palabra replaces a text
translation provider just because its integrated audio service works. Configured
coverage is not live verification of every language pair, and a short generated
audio diagnostic is not verification of a two-person browser call. Further paid
tests need explicit approval.

Langpretation must translate continuously while the user is still speaking, and
the listener should hear the translation in the speaker's own voice, not just a
generic AI voice.

**Why:** The owner explicitly clarified that a phrase-upload bridge and standard
synthetic speech do not fulfill the intended Nanivio experience.

**How to apply:** Treat continuous streaming and speaker identity as separate
acceptance criteria. A voice-profile type definition is not a working clone.
Palabra's per-session voice preservation is experimental and needs an initial
adaptation period; do not present it as an instant or permanent personal voice.
Obtain the speaker's permission and verify similarity through a consented real
listening test before claiming this requirement is fully complete.

Retain the current voice-preservation approach as the owner-approved starting
point for international live calls.

**Why:** The owner compared the generated English reference with the later French
voice-preservation sample and answered “Sounds close.”

**How to apply:** This is subjective approval of that sample only, not proof of
an exact human voice clone, instant matching, all-language quality, or a working
two-account browser call. Preserve the consent and adaptation disclosures.
